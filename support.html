Support

Audio 2-Axis Level is a level you listen to. Lay your iPhone face down on the thing you are leveling, tap Start sound, and adjust until you hear the steady top tone. Your eyes stay on the job.

Quick answers

It says I am off level on a surface I know is level.
Every iPhone's sensors have a small offset of their own. On a surface you know is level, tap Zero both. The app remembers it.
Which way is high?
The bubble on the dial floats toward the high side, like a real vial. X is toward/away from you (+ means the far edge is higher); Y is left/right (+ means your right-hand edge is higher).
Face down or face up?
Face down is best: the screen side is flat, the back has the camera bump. Face up works for leveling side to side (Y only) when you want to see the screen.
The sound stopped when the screen went dark.
It should not — the sound keeps playing with the screen locked. If it does stop, check that the Ring/Silent switch and Focus modes are not silencing media, and that the volume is up.
Start when face down.
Tap it, lay the phone face down, and the sound starts by itself once the phone has been still for a second. It stops when you turn the phone face up. Arming lasts one minute.
The Digi-Pas DWL-90Pro cube will not connect.
Is "BT" showing on the cube (hold Alt. Zero, then press Power once)? Is another device holding it? Is the battery under 2.5 V? Then Settings → Sensors → Scan again and tap the cube.
