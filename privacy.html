Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Audio 2-Axis Level does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

  • The app reads your iPhone's or iPad's motion sensors (gravity direction only), or optionally a Digi-Pas DWL-90Pro digital level over Bluetooth, to measure tilt. Those readings are shown and turned into sound on your device only. They are never stored and never sent anywhere.
  • Settings you change (tolerances, zero offsets, which cube to connect to) are stored only on your device.
  • The app uses no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and makes no network connections.
  • Bluetooth permission is requested solely to talk to the Digi-Pas cube.
